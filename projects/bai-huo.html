Bai Huo

Bai Huo is a phygital exhibition exploring how everyday objects trigger emotional memories through sight, smell, habit, and time. Visitors navigate a maze of four life stages — Childhood, Youth, Adulthood, and Wisdom — discovering familiar objects and unlocking poetic digital stories through QR interactions.
