Autour des Murs

Autour des Murs explores the creative ecosystem of the 11th arrondissement of Paris, mapping five pillars: galleries, studios, street art, cultural organizations, and community spaces. Through illustrated visuals, it reveals connections supporting the local arts economy, highlighting collaboration networks and cultural impact within a vibrant, interconnected creative community.
